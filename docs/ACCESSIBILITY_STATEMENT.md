# Accessibility Statement — AcademicCheck AI

AcademicCheck AI aims to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. As of 6 September 2026 this repository has automated source and fixture checks, and Playwright axe coverage for public marketing/auth routes. It is **not** a completed AA certificate.

Known limitations: PDF reports are not tagged; screen-reader walkthroughs (NVDA, VoiceOver, JAWS) have not been recorded; Lighthouse has not been run on a production URL; `/app/compare` and `/admin` are not routes (use Versions and `/app/admin`).

Contact the operator if you cannot use a page. Do not treat this statement as a legal AA declaration until the AT and Lighthouse evidence is attached.
